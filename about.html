Our History

Our Story

We are a local family owned and operated bakery in operation since 1942. Founded in historic Coventry, RI Our roots we're laid down during the 2nd World War where we began serving our community.

We have passed down the traditions of making fresh baked goods and serving our community over generations bonding people together and breaking bread with one another(Literally!)

Where Our Ingredients Come From

We source our ingredients from the same community we serve ensuring everything we make is fresh and with love

  • Dairy: from Knotty Oak Farms
  • Fruit: That changes with the seasons
  • Flour: from the local Coventry Mills
